# Daily loop

Mode: Operate. Scope: the pass in all its states, game introductions, the speed game, the demo and practice round, and the set's end. Her job is to finish today's set alone. Getting lost is the main risk.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pass is both home and map. She leaves one ticket to play a game and always returns to the same ticket, with the next row lit. It refuses the category's dashboard of tiles, streaks and scores.

OWN-WORLD: Dark navy #0E1B2C surround, one white panel, amber #FFB000 held on the current row and nowhere else as a state, navy text on both. Be Vietnam Pro, tabular numerals, heavy dividing rules, a perforated tear line with punched notches. Every button is a filled or outlined shape with words. No red, no planes, no barcodes, no flight wording.

STORY: She understands what today holds, sees where she is, and presses the one obvious button. After each game she sees her row checked and the amber band move down. When all rows are checked the stub says done for today.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Greeting top left and a worded Settings button top right. Below, the white pass fills the screen: head row with today's set and date, then numbered rows (number, game name, minutes), the current row in amber. A perforated tear line, then a stub holding one full-width navy button with its word and arrow, within thumb reach. Under the pass, the week in words. Signature interaction: the amber band moves slowly from the finished row to the next one, once, with reduced-motion handled by an instant change.
